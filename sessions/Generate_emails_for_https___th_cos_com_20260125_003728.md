**COS Brand Pillars**:
- **Contemporary minimalism**: Clean lines, functional luxury, timeless design
- **Scandinavian heritage**: Subtle elegance, quality craftsmanship, restraint over excess

- **Performance drivers**: High open rates (30%+), click-through (5%+), mobile-first
