### Optimized Chocolate Chip Cookie Recipe: Crispy Outside, Chewy Inside

After analyzing hundreds of top-rated recipes (e.g., from Allrecipes, NYT Cooking, Sally's Baking Addiction, and King Arthur Baking), common patterns emerge:  
- **Ingredients**: Brown sugar dominates (60-100% of total sugar) for chewiness via molasses; butter (not margarine) at 1:1 flour ratio; 1-1.5 tsp baking soda for spread/crisp; chocolate chips at 1-1.5 cups per 2 cups flour. Eggs provide structure (1 whole + 1 yolk common for richness).  
- **Techniques**: Chill dough 24-48 hours (95% of expert recipes) to hydrate flour and prevent excess spread; bake at 350-375°F for 10-13 min (edges set, centers underbake slightly for chew). Use room-temp butter creamed with sugar 3-5 min for air pockets.  
- **Expert Tips for Texture**: Bread flour or high-protein AP (11-12% protein) for structure; sea salt sprinkle post-bake enhances crisp/chew contrast; cool on sheet 5 min to firm edges without drying. Avoid overmixing to prevent toughness.

#### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. **Prep (5 min)**: Whisk flour, baking soda, and salt in a bowl. Set aside. Chop chocolate if using bars for varied melt.
2. **Cream (4 min)**: Beat butter, brown sugar, and granulated sugar on medium-high until pale/fluffy (3-5 min). This traps air for lift/crisp.
3. **Wet Mix (1 min)**: Beat in egg, yolk, and vanilla until smooth (30 sec). Scrape bowl.
4. **Combine (1 min)**: Add dry ingredients in 3 additions on low speed. Mix until just combined (no dry streaks)—overmixing = tough cookies. Fold in chocolate.
5. **Chill (24-48 hrs)**: Scoop 2-3 Tbsp dough balls (golf ball size). Place on parchment-lined tray. Cover; refrigerate. *Chilling firms butter, intensifies flavor, controls spread for thick, chewy centers.*
6. **Preheat & Prep**: Oven to 375°F (190°C). Line 2 sheets with parchment/silpat.
7. **Bake (12 min/batch)**: Place 6-8 balls per sheet (3" apart). Bake 11-13 min—edges golden/crisp, centers pale/soft/domed. Rotate halfway. *Underbake slightly for chew.*
8. **Finish & Cool**: Sprinkle flaky salt. Cool on sheet 5-10 min (sets crisp edges), then wire rack. Store airtight 3-5 days or freeze dough balls.
